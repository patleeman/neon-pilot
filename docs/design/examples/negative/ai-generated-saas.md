# Negative Anchor: AI-Generated SaaS Styling

Avoid UI that reads as a generic AI product or SaaS dashboard.

## Fails Because

- Purple/blue gradients, glow, bokeh, and glassy panels become decorative chrome.
- Oversized rounded cards and atmospheric backgrounds replace real hierarchy.
- Decorative status pills compete with working controls.
- The UI feels branded independently from Neon Pilot.

## Prefer Instead

- Neutral IDE/workbench surfaces.
- Selection, focus, and status color used sparingly and semantically.
- Hierarchy from layout, alignment, typography, and compact grouping.
